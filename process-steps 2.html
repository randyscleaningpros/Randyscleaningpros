Our 🔟 10-Step Gold Standard Process

💚 Same steps. Every room. Every visit. Here's exactly what we do, step by step.

1️⃣

🧴 Fill the Steamer & Choose Attachments

We start by filling the steamer and picking the right attachment for the job. Having everything ready before we start saves time and keeps us moving smoothly through your home.

2️⃣

🧺 Clear & Organize the Counters

We remove everything from counters and surfaces and put items together safely in one place — so nothing gets lost and every surface underneath gets fully cleaned.

3️⃣

🧪 Apply the Right Cleaner to the Right Surface

We never spray one cleaner on every surface. We identify the material first, then apply a cleaner that's appropriate for it — and let it dwell for a few minutes so the chemistry can do the hard work.

✅ Fiberglass & acrylic showers✅ Ceramic & porcelain tile✅ Toilets✅ Compatible sink basins

🚫 Never on natural stone, unfinished wood, or delicate finishes.

4️⃣

🌬️ Dust High to Low

Dust always falls, so we dust ceilings, fans, and shelves first — before anything below them gets touched.

5️⃣

🌀 Vacuum Loose Dust, Hair & Debris

We vacuum baseboards, corners, around the toilet, hair in the shower, the exhaust fan cover, and the floor. Removing loose debris first means less mess and fewer used towels for everything after.

6️⃣

💨 Steam Clean Appropriate Surfaces

This is where our signature steam advantage comes in. We steam only where it adds real value — never every square inch.

🚿 Showers🛁 Tubs🧱 Grout🚰 Around faucets🚽 Toilet hinges🧼 Sink edges🎚️ Knobs📏 Tracks
7️⃣

✋ Feel Every Surface by Hand

Your hand catches what your eyes miss — soap scum, sticky residue, rough mineral deposits, and grime that's still hanging on. If it doesn't feel clean, it isn't clean yet.

8️⃣

🪥 Lightly Scrub Only Where Needed

We let the cleaner and steam do the work first, and only scrub the stubborn spots that still need it. Less aggressive scrubbing means better protection for your surfaces.

9️⃣

🧻 Wipe Clean & Dry With Premium Microfiber

Premium microfiber traps dirt instead of pushing it around, leaving every surface polished, dry, and streak-free.

🔟

🦠 Disinfect & Final Gold Standard Inspection

Once every surface is clean and completely dry, we apply our professional no-residue disinfectant according to the label and allow the full required contact time before it air-dries — so it's not just clean, it's disinfected. Then we do a final walkthrough: flush the toilet, check for drips, check the mirror from different angles, turn on every light, and run a hand over the sink, vanity, and shower one last time.

🚫 A quick note: some materials should never be steamed — unsealed wood, some delicate finishes, electronics, and other heat-sensitive materials. We always identify the surface first and choose the method that's actually right for it.
