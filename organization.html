Our Organization Process

Careful, practical and built around your home.

1

Empty & Evaluate

When appropriate, we remove manageable items so we can see the space and understand what we are working with.

2

Sort

Items are grouped into logical categories so you can clearly see what you have.

3

You Decide

If something may be donated, discarded, relocated or kept, you make the final decision. We never throw away your belongings without your permission.

4

Clean

Once an area is emptied, we can clean accessible shelves, drawers and surfaces before putting items back.

5

Organize

Items are arranged around frequency of use, available space and what makes the most sense for your household.

6

Put Everything Back

Everything gets its place. The goal is not just to look good for a photo — it is to create a system you can actually live with.

Why organization costs more

Professional organization takes considerably more time than ordinary cleaning. We are sorting belongings, creating categories, evaluating space, cleaning emptied areas, developing a practical layout and carefully putting everything back.

A small space may take an hour or two. A heavily cluttered pantry, closet or room can take several hours.

Storage Products

Use what you have — or add organizing products.

We can incorporate bins, baskets, drawer organizers, shelf organizers, cabinet organizers, non-slip liners, labels, pantry containers and other storage solutions.

Storage products and materials are separate. They are not included in the organization price unless specifically stated in your quote. You may purchase your own products, or we can discuss what would work best for your space.
