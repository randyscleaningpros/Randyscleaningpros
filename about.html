Meet the Owner

Hi, I’m Randy. I built this company around caring enough to notice.

My goal is simple: deliver the kind of thorough, respectful cleaning I would want in my own home.

Why I started Randy Premier Cleaning

I want customers to feel the difference between “cleaned” and truly cared for.

I pay close attention to dust, hair, edges, materials and the small details that are easy to miss. I believe professionalism means using the right tool for the job, communicating clearly and treating every home with respect.

That is why I created the Gold Standard system: a structured way to keep the quality consistent instead of relying on memory or rushing from one task to the next.

I aim for five-star work, but more importantly, I want customers to know I care about the result and will keep improving the system as the company grows.
