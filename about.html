Meet the Owner

Hi, I'm Randy.

The story behind Randy's Premier Cleaning — and why it's more than a cleaning company to me.

My name is Randy, and I started Randy's Premier Cleaning because I believe homeowners deserve better.

I believe every home should be treated with the same care and respect I'd give my own. To me, cleaning isn't just about making a house look tidy for a day — it's about genuinely caring for the place where you and your family live.

That's why I built my Gold Standard Cleaning System: a consistent, detail-oriented process that means every customer gets the same professional experience, every single visit — not just when things happen to go well.

Honestly, my favorite part of this job is seeing someone feel proud of their home again. That feeling is what keeps me going.

My goal has never been to just clean houses — it's to build real relationships. When you work with us, you're not just a job on a schedule. You become part of our family, and every home we walk into matters to me just as much as the last.

My hope is for Randy's Premier Cleaning to become the most trusted residential cleaning company in Maine — one home, one customer, and one honest day's work at a time.

— Randy
