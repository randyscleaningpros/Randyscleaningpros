About Us

A personal cleaning company built around care, knowledge, honesty, and long-term trust.

Hi, I’m Randy, the founder of Randy Premier Cleaning.

I started this company because I believe your home deserves more than a quick cleaning. It deserves someone who genuinely cares about protecting your home, respecting your belongings, and treating every room with the same care and attention I would give my own.

Cleaning, to me, is both an art and a responsibility. That is why I developed a 10-Step Cleaning System to deliver consistent, detail-focused results every time. My goal is not only to leave your home looking clean, but also to help protect the surfaces, finishes, and materials that make your home unique.

I carefully choose the right products for each surface because the wrong cleaner can damage natural stone, hardwood, tile, countertops, and other delicate materials.

Your family’s health is just as important to me as the cleanliness of your home. Whenever possible, I use products that are effective while being mindful of the air you breathe and the surfaces your family touches.

When a demanding condition requires a stronger cleaning solution, I explain why it is needed before beginning, answer questions, ask for approval, and provide recommended ventilation or waiting periods.

As Randy Premier Cleaning grows, I plan to build a team of professional Cleaning Techs who share the same values: honesty, attention to detail, respect for every home, and a genuine passion for exceptional service.

Thank you for considering Randy Premier Cleaning. I look forward to earning your trust and helping make your home cleaner, healthier, and more enjoyable.

— Randy, Founder
