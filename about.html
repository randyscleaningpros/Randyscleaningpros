Meet the Owner

Hi, I'm Randy.

The story behind Randy's Premier Cleaning — and why it's more than a cleaning company to me.

My name is Randy, and I started Randy's Premier Cleaning because cleaning is more than just a job to me. I genuinely care about the homes I work in, the people who live there, and the feeling they have when the job is finished.

When someone trusts me with their home, I take that personally. Your home is where you relax, spend time with family, make memories, and live your everyday life. I believe it deserves to be treated with care, respect, and real attention to detail.

I don't want to simply wipe down surfaces, vacuum a floor, and move on. I put pride and heart into my work. I want you to notice the difference when you walk through your home after a cleaning.

I aim for one reaction every time: “WOW.”

I don't want you to think, “Yeah, it looks clean.” I want you to see the details, feel how fresh and cared-for your home is, and notice things you may not have expected us to clean.

If I don't earn a WOW, I don't feel like I've done my job.

Every home is different, so great cleaning starts with paying attention. We focus on the details, remove dust instead of simply moving it around, choose methods that make sense for the surface, and take the time to care for areas that are easy to overlook.

Whether it's a regular cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in or move-out service, organization project, Airbnb turnover, or specialty cleaning, my goal stays the same: leave the home better than we found it.

I'm building Randy's Premier Cleaning around one simple idea: care about the work. Customers are not just appointments on a schedule, and homes are not just another job. People are trusting us with their personal space, and that deserves honesty, respect, effort, and genuine care.

I want every customer to know there is a real person behind this company who truly cares about whether they are happy. I listen, I pay attention, I aim to please, and I will always keep looking for ways to do better.

Right now, I personally do the cleaning myself. As Randy's Premier Cleaning grows, I will be extremely selective about who I trust to represent this company and enter our customers' homes. Every team member will be carefully chosen, required to pass a thorough background check and driver's license/driving-record check when driving is part of the job, and trained directly by my side. My goal is simple: no matter who arrives at your home, they will be trained to follow the same detailed standards, care, respect, and cleaning process that make up the Randy's Premier Cleaning way.

More than anything, I want you to walk through your freshly cleaned home, look around, smile, and say:

“WOW.”

That's the Gold Standard I'm working toward every single time.

— Randy
Owner, Randy's Premier Cleaning
