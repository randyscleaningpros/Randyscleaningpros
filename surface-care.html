Surface Knowledge Matters

The right result starts with knowing what you’re touching.

We do not treat every countertop, floor, cabinet or fixture the same. Material and finish come first.

SurfaceOur ApproachWhat We Avoid
Natural stone / marblepH-appropriate, non-abrasive products; soft microfiberHarsh acids, unknown chemicals, aggressive pads
Tile & groutCleaner, steam or agitation chosen for tile/grout typeOver-aggressive tools on delicate finishes
Finished woodControlled moisture, finish-compatible products; optional conditioningSoaking, harsh degreasers, steam unless specifically approved
Laminate / LVPLow-moisture cleaning and approved floor productsStanding water and excessive steam
Stainless steelClean with the grain and polish when appropriateSteel wool and cross-grain abrasion
Glass & mirrorsLow-residue cleaner and lint-free microfiberGreasy towels and excessive product
Painted surfacesGentle spot cleaning after compatibility checkSaturation and harsh solvents
ElectronicsDry or manufacturer-approved methods onlySteam, saturation and sprayed liquid
