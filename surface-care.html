Surface-Safe Cleaning

The right cleaner, towel, brush, pad, and technique can make a major difference.

SurfaceOur approachWe avoid
Natural stone / marblepH-appropriate, non-abrasive products and soft microfiberHarsh acids, abrasive pads, unknown chemicals
TileCleaner and agitation matched to tile and grout typeOverly aggressive tools on delicate finishes
WoodControlled moisture, soft microfiber, finish-conscious productsSoaking, harsh degreasers, abrasive scrubbing
LaminateLow-moisture cleaning and gentle padsStanding water and scratch-prone abrasives
Stainless steelClean with the grain, then polish when appropriateSteel wool and random cross-grain scrubbing
GlassLow-residue cleaner with lint-free microfiberGreasy cloths and excessive product

Note: specialty restoration, sealing, refinishing, or damage repair should be evaluated before service.
