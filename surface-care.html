Surface-aware cleaning

We look before we scrub.

A powerful product is not automatically a better product. We choose surface-appropriate methods and test when needed.

STONE CARE

Clean. Protect. Preserve.

For compatible porous stone, sealing can help resist staining and moisture penetration. We treat sealing as a separate service because the stone, finish, existing sealer and condition matter.

Our rule

Identify → test → clean → inspect → protect.

Protection products are used only when they are compatible with the specific material and finish. When we are unsure, we check the manufacturer or recommend a specialist rather than guessing.
