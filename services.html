Services

Complete care for the home — with the right tool for each job.

Our service is built around detail and judgment. We vacuum, wipe, scrub, mop, steam and disinfect where each method belongs.

🏡

Standard House Cleaning

Top-to-bottom dusting and vacuum capture, surface cleaning, bathroom care, kitchen care, detail touch points and floor cleaning.

✨

Deep Cleaning

For heavier buildup or reset cleans. Adds more detail time, powered scrubbing and steam use on compatible surfaces where it improves the result.

🔁

Recurring Cleaning

Consistent room-by-room maintenance so dust, hair and buildup do not get a chance to take over again.

🚚

Move-In / Move-Out

Detailed empty-home cleaning with optional interiors of cabinets, refrigerator, oven and other areas.

🏠

Airbnb Turnover

Guest-ready cleaning, linens/towels as arranged, trash, kitchen, bathroom, inspection and restocking when supplies are provided.

🏢

Small Office Cleaning

Professional recurring or one-time care for smaller workspaces, restrooms, break areas, floors and touch points.

📦

Home Organization

Pantry, cabinets, closets, refrigerator, laundry supplies, linens, playrooms and whole-home organization.

🔥

Whole-Home Steam Add-On

For customers who want a more steam-focused, lower-chemical approach. Steam is only used on materials that can safely handle heat and moisture.

🧽

Appliance & Detail Cleaning

Inside refrigerator, oven, toaster oven, microwave and other safe-to-clean surfaces. Utility pipes and boiler exteriors may be surface-cleaned only when cool and safe — this is not mechanical servicing.

🚗

Interior Car Detailing

Vacuuming, crevice detailing, interior surface care and upholstery-safe cleaning by custom quote.

🌿

Exterior / Outdoor Detail Work

Selected outside cleaning projects can be quoted separately based on access, surface type and equipment needed.

🪵

Wood Care Add-On

Optional conditioning or appearance care for compatible sealed wood after the surface and finish are identified.

Important: we do not promise to steam every surface. Natural stone, unfinished or unsealed wood, delicate finishes, electronics and other heat- or moisture-sensitive materials require a different method.
