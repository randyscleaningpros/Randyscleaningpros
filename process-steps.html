The Full Breakdown

Our 10-Step Gold Standard Process

Every visit, every home, the same repeatable system — here's exactly what happens, step by step.

1

🏡 Every Cleaning Starts With Protection

Before we touch a single surface, we carefully identify the materials throughout your home. Using the wrong cleaner can dull finishes, damage sealers, or shorten the life of certain materials — so we always choose products appropriate for the surface we're cleaning.

🪵 Finished Wood🪨 Granite✨ Marble💎 Quartz🪟 Glass🧊 Stainless Steel🧱 Tile🏠 Luxury Vinyl Plank
2

🌀 We Remove Dust Before We Clean

Dust shouldn't just get pushed around — it should be removed. Using our professional Dyson vacuum with soft dusting attachments, we work around each room from left to right, removing dust instead of blowing it into the air.

FurnitureShelvesBaseboardsWindow sillsBlindsCeiling fansVentsDécorCorners
3

🧽 We Let Science Do the Hard Work

Rather than immediately scrubbing every surface, we apply professional cleaning products where appropriate and give them time to loosen grease, soap scum, and everyday buildup — for better cleaning, less aggressive scrubbing, and better protection for your home's surfaces.

4

🛁 Deep Bathroom Restoration

Bathrooms get extra attention because they work the hardest. We thoroughly clean showers, tubs, sinks, toilets, tile, grout, fixtures, and hard-to-reach areas to remove soap scum, body oils, and everyday buildup.

5

🍳 Kitchen Detailing

Grease, food residue, fingerprints, appliances, cabinets, backsplashes, counters — every kitchen deserves professional attention, and that's exactly what it gets.

6

🛋️ Living Spaces

Dust, furniture, shelves, electronics, décor, light switches, baseboards — we focus on the details many companies overlook.

7

🛏️ Bedrooms

Fresh. Comfortable. Dust-free. Relaxing. A bedroom should feel like a place to unwind, and we treat it that way.

8

🧹 Floors

Different floors require different care. Whether your home has hardwood, tile, luxury vinyl, laminate, or other flooring, we choose products and techniques appropriate for your floor type to leave them beautifully clean.

9

✨ The Finishing Touches

Before we leave, we handle the little details that make the biggest difference.

Polish mirrorsPolish stainless steelClean trash cansStraighten areasRemove fingerprintsWipe final smudges
10

🔦 The Premier Final Inspection

This is one of our favorite steps. Using a professional flashlight, we inspect your home for dust, hair, streaks, smudges, and missed details. If we find something, we fix it before we leave — because our reputation is built one home at a time.
