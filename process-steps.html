Our Gold Standard
Cleaning Process

10 simple steps. Every room. Every visit. Here's exactly how we do it.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
1

⭐ Step 1: Prepare

🛠️ We inspect each room and prepare the right tools, products, and equipment for the job.

2

⭐ Step 2: Clear the Area

📦 We remove clutter and trash so every surface can be cleaned properly.

3

⭐ Step 3: Pre-Treat Surfaces

🧴 We apply the appropriate cleaners and allow them time to loosen dirt, grease, and grime.

4

⭐ Step 4: Remove High Dust

🪶 We dust ceiling fans, vents, light fixtures, cobwebs, and other high areas first.

5

⭐ Step 5: Vacuum All Dry Debris

🌪️ We vacuum dust, pet hair, crumbs, lint, loose dirt, floors, furniture, corners, and other appropriate dry surfaces before wet cleaning begins.

6

⭐ Step 6: Steam Clean

♨️ When appropriate, we use high-temperature steam to help loosen buildup and clean hard-to-reach areas.

7

⭐ Step 7: Clean & Detail

✨ We wipe, scrub when needed, and carefully clean every surface from top to bottom.

8

⭐ Step 8: Disinfect

🦠 We disinfect appropriate high-touch surfaces according to the disinfectant manufacturer's directions.

9

⭐ Step 9: Finish the Room

🏡 We mop hard floors, replace items neatly, and leave every room looking clean, fresh, and organized.

10

⭐ Step 10: Final Inspection

🏆 Before we leave, we inspect every room to make sure it meets our Gold Standard.
