Our Process

Cleaning is more than a service. It is an art, a science, and a responsibility.

We do not simply make a home look clean. We work to protect it.

Every home is unique, and every surface deserves the proper care. Our 10-Step Cleaning System is designed to deliver consistent results while helping preserve the beauty and longevity of your home.

We clean with knowledge, not guesswork.

Using the wrong cleaner may wear down finishes, dull natural shine, damage sealers, leave marks, or shorten the life of expensive surfaces. We carefully select products and techniques for each material.

1. Review

Confirm priorities, instructions, access details, and sensitive surfaces.

2. Prepare

Choose the correct tools, towels, and solutions for the home.

3. High-to-Low

Work in a controlled order to avoid spreading dust back onto finished areas.

4. Detail

Focus on touch points, edges, fixtures, handles, and buildup.

5. Surface Care

Match products and techniques to stone, tile, wood, metal, and glass.

6. Kitchen Care

Clean food-preparation and appliance surfaces carefully.

7. Bathroom Care

Avoid products that may harm stone, sealers, or finishes.

8. Floors

Vacuum and clean floors using material-appropriate methods.

9. Finishing Touches

Tidy, align, polish, and present each room professionally.

10. Final Inspection

Review the work and correct missed details before leaving.
