The Science of Clean

Why Our Process Actually Works

Cleaning isn't just wiping — it's chemistry and sequence. We identify every surface first because the wrong product can dull a finish or damage a sealer in seconds. We remove dust before we ever spray, because pushing dust around with a wet cloth just relocates it, while vacuuming lifts it out for good. We apply our cleaning solution and let it dwell for a few minutes because the surfactants need time to chemically break down grease and soap scum — which means less aggressive scrubbing and better protection for your surfaces. Then we work top to bottom and dry to wet, so nothing we've already cleaned gets re-contaminated on the way down. That's the science behind the shine: a documented 10-Step Gold Standard System — not guesswork.
