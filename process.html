🔬 The Science of Clean

Why Our Process Actually Works

  • 🎯 We identify every surface first — the wrong product can dull a finish or damage a sealer in seconds.
  • 🌀 We remove dust before we ever spray — pushing dust around with a wet cloth just relocates it, vacuuming lifts it out for good.
  • 💨 We steam where it's appropriate — kitchens and bathrooms get professional steam to loosen grease, soap scum, and body oils from hard surfaces like showers, tubs, and grout.
  • 🦠 Then we disinfect — once everything is clean and completely dry, we apply a professional no-residue disinfectant and give it the proper contact time.
  • 🔝 We work top to bottom, dry to wet — so nothing we've already cleaned gets re-contaminated on the way down.

That's the science behind the shine — a documented 10-Step Gold Standard System. ⭐ Not guesswork.
