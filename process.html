Dusting Method Matters

Why we vacuum first — not just wipe.

A duster or dry towel can move dust off a surface and straight into the air you breathe, where it just resettles somewhere else. A vacuum with a soft dusting brush lifts dust and hair out of the room entirely. That's why vacuuming leads our process instead of coming last.
