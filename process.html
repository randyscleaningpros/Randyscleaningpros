How We Clean

Every visit follows the same proven routine.

We spray our cleaning solution in bathrooms, kitchens, and on floors and let it break down grime for a few minutes. Depending on how much buildup there is, we scrub away soap scum from showers, tubs, sinks, and toilets — including the tight cracks and corners — then disinfect and polish before we do a final walkthrough.

🧴

Spray & Dwell

Our degreasing cleaner goes on bathroom, kitchen, and floor surfaces first and is given a few minutes to break down grease and grime before we touch it.

🪥

Scrub Where It Counts

Soap scum and buildup are scrubbed from showers, tubs, sinks, and around the toilet — including the cracks and corners that get missed.

🦠

Disinfect Everything

High-touch surfaces, bathrooms, and trash cans are disinfected as a standard part of every visit, not an upgrade.

✨

Polish & Shine

Stainless steel gets polished to a shine and sinks are buffed until they gleam — small touches that make a big difference.
