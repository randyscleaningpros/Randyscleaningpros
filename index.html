🏆 Our Mission

Setting the Gold Standard in Residential Cleaning

At Randy's Premier Cleaning, our mission is to set the Gold Standard in residential cleaning by delivering consistent, detail-oriented service using professional equipment, surface-specific products, and our proven Gold Standard Cleaning System. We don't just clean homes — we care for them. Every visit follows the same systematic process to deliver consistent, professional results our customers can depend on.

Our Signature Advantage

🔥 Steam Cleans & Sanitizes at the Same Time. Then We Disinfect.

Here's what makes us different from a typical cleaning company: our high-temperature steam doesn't just loosen grease and soap scum — it sanitizes surfaces as we clean them, in the same step. In kitchens and bathrooms — where it makes sense — we use professional steam on appropriate hard surfaces like showers, tubs, grout, faucet bases, and sink edges, so you're getting a deeper, more effective clean than a spray bottle and a rag alone.

💨

Steam Loosens the Grime

Heat softens grease and soap scum so it lifts away with far less scrubbing — and reaches into grout lines and corners a sponge can't.

🦠

Cleans & Sanitizes Together

High-temperature steam sanitizes appropriate surfaces at the same time it lifts dirt and grime — one step doing double duty, which is what makes our process more effective than standard wipe-and-spray cleaning.

🧴

Then We Fully Disinfect

Once a surface is fully cleaned and dried, we still apply a professional, no-residue disinfectant according to its label directions and give it the proper contact time before it air-dries — for complete protection, not just a quick pass.

🎯

Right Method, Right Surface

Steam isn't for everything. We identify each surface first and only steam where it's appropriate — never on unsealed wood, delicate finishes, electronics, or other heat-sensitive materials.
