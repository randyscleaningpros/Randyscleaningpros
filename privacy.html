Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

At Randy Premier Cleaning, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We only collect information necessary to provide cleaning services, prepare estimates, communicate with customers, and improve our business.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Information about the cleaning services you request

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Provide free estimates
  • Schedule appointments
  • Communicate regarding your service
  • Send appointment reminders
  • Improve customer service

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your information is kept private and is only shared when required by law or when necessary to complete the services you request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information. While no online system can guarantee complete security, we work hard to safeguard the information you provide.

Cookies

Our website may use standard website technologies, such as cookies, to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our website.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request updates or removal of your personal information from our records whenever legally permitted.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Randy Premier Cleaning.
